Method III
P0: KanR-P_ind(IPTG)-ccdB
P1: AmpR-Cas9-sg(ccdB)
P2: AmpR-Cas9-sg(GFP)
NT=P0+P2
T=P0+P1
Cultured on: Amp/IPTG, Amp/Kan/IPTG

The core of Method III: P0 both rescues (KanR) and kills (P_ind(IPTG)-ccdB) the host under different conditions (Kan; IPTG) and combination of conditions (Kan/IPTG)
The flaw of Method III: P0 and P1 are dissociated (the specific selection for P1 doesn't guarantee P1+P0 dual-plasmid transformed survivors); CRISPR couldn't achieve 100% efficiency (a technically unavoidable reality); the half-lives of expression products of resistance markers introduce timelag that interferes with results (also a technically unavoidable reality; could be partially avoided by raising Kan concentraion or prolonging recovery)

The dilemma: association of P0 to P1 can be achieved by adding a toxic gene to P1 and a corresponding rescue gene to P0; but that extra introduction of the P1 toxic gene results in the loss of observation that P0 loss rescues/kills the host under different conditions, since P0 loss directly leads to the release of P1 toxicity.
